## 🏆 Part 5: The Product Hunt Winning Formula (#1 Ranking Algorithm)

Product Hunt ranking is **not** decided by whoever gets the most spam upvotes. Their anti-fraud algorithm discounts new accounts and penalizes suspicious spikes. Here is how to legitimately win #1:

### Rule 1: Launch at Exactly 12:01 AM PST
- Product Hunt's leaderboard resets at **12:00 AM PST (Pacific Standard Time)**.
- Launching at 12:01 AM PST gives your product a full **24 hours** to accumulate votes across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

### Rule 2: Never Say "Upvote Me" (Instant Algorithm Penalty)
- Product Hunt actively monitors social media and emails for phrases like *"Please upvote me on Product Hunt"*.
- **Always say:**  
  *"We just launched App Launch OS on Product Hunt! I’d love to get your honest feedback and hear your thoughts in the comments."*
- Asking for feedback or discussion increases your ranking score; asking for upvotes gets your votes muted.

### Rule 3: Reply to Every Single Comment Within 10 Minutes
- The Product Hunt algorithm rewards active Maker engagement.
- If you get 80 comments and you reply with thoughtful, detailed answers to all 80, your engagement score doubles. Treat the comment section like an active community town hall.

### Rule 4: Tap Active Product Hunt Accounts First
- Votes from users who created their account *today* carry almost zero algorithmic weight.
- Reach out to friends, fellow indie hackers, and Twitter/LinkedIn contacts who already have established Product Hunt accounts with activity and badges.
