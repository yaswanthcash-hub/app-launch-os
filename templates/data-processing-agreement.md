## Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (“**DPA**”) forms part of the agreement dated
`[DATE]` between:

- **Controller:** `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, with an address at `[ADDRESS]`
  (“**Controller**”); and
- **Processor:** `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, with an address at `[ADDRESS]`
  (“**Processor**”).

Controller and Processor may each be a “**Party**” and together the
“**Parties**.”

## 1. Scope and purpose

Processor will process personal data on behalf of Controller only to provide
`[DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE]` under the master agreement identified as
`[MASTER AGREEMENT]` (the “**Agreement**”). The subject matter, duration,
nature, purpose, categories of data subjects, categories of personal data, and
special categories of data are described in Schedule 1.

Processor will not determine the purposes and means of processing except as
permitted by applicable law or expressly authorized in writing by Controller. If
Processor believes an instruction infringes applicable data-protection law,
Processor will notify Controller promptly and may suspend the affected
processing until the issue is resolved, to the extent permitted by the Agreement
and law.

## 2. Documented instructions

Processor will process personal data only on documented instructions from
Controller, including instructions concerning transfers of personal data to a
third country or international organization, unless required by applicable law.
The Parties' agreed instructions are set out in Schedule 1 and may be updated
through `[CHANGE METHOD]`.

Processor will inform Controller before legally required processing, unless
prohibited by law on important grounds of public interest or another applicable
basis.

## 3. Confidentiality

Processor will ensure that persons authorized to process personal data are bound
by confidentiality obligations or appropriate statutory duties. Access is
limited to personnel who need access to perform authorized services and is
reviewed at least `[FREQUENCY]`.

## 4. Security of processing

Taking into account the state of the art, implementation costs, the nature,
scope, context, and purposes of processing, and the varying likelihood and
severity of risk to individuals, Processor will implement and maintain the
technical and organizational measures described in Schedule 2.

The measures must address, as applicable:

- pseudonymization and encryption;
- confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing
  systems;
- restoration of availability and access after an incident;
- access control, authentication, authorization, and privilege management;
- secure development, testing, change management, and vulnerability management;
- logging, monitoring, and review;
- secure deletion, return, and media handling;
- business continuity, backup, and disaster recovery; and
- regular testing, assessment, and evaluation of effectiveness.

These measures are not a warranty or a representation that a particular legal
standard is satisfied.

## 5. Subprocessors

Controller generally authorizes Processor to engage the subprocessors listed in
Schedule 3. Processor will impose obligations on subprocessors that provide a
level of protection materially consistent with this DPA, including
data-security, confidentiality, assistance, deletion, audit, and transfer
obligations as applicable.

Processor remains responsible to Controller for the performance of authorized
subprocessors to the extent stated in the Agreement and applicable law.
Processor will provide notice of an intended addition or replacement at least
`[NOTICE PERIOD]` before it takes effect, using `[NOTICE METHOD]`. Controller
may object within `[OBJECTION PERIOD]` on reasonable data-protection grounds;
the Parties will work in good faith on a workaround or termination option.

## 6. Data-subject requests and Controller assistance

Taking into account the nature of processing and information available,
Processor will assist Controller through appropriate technical and
organizational measures to respond to requests exercising applicable rights,
including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection,
consent withdrawal, and review of automated decisions where applicable.

Processor will promptly notify Controller if it receives a request directly from
a data subject and will not respond except on Controller's documented
instructions or as required by law.

## 7. Security incidents and personal-data breaches

Processor will notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a
personal-data breach affecting processed personal data. The notice will include,
to the extent known:

- the nature and categories of affected data and data subjects;
- the likely consequences;
- the date, duration, and circumstances;
- containment and mitigation measures;
- contact details for the incident lead; and
- information needed for Controller to meet its own notification obligations.

Processor will provide reasonable updates and a final incident report. Processor
will not notify a regulator, data subject, or other third party about
Controller's processing unless instructed by Controller or required by law.
Notification timeframes and escalation contacts are specified in Schedule 4.

## 8. Data-protection impact assessments and prior consultation

Processor will assist Controller, where applicable, with data-protection impact
assessments, prior consultations, regulator inquiries, and demonstrations of
compliance by providing information about processing and security measures.
Processor will notify Controller before making a material change to the
processing that could affect an assessment or consultation.

## 9. Audits and inspections

Processor will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate
compliance with this DPA and allow for and contribute to audits and inspections
by Controller or an independent auditor mandated by Controller, subject to
reasonable notice, confidentiality, security, scope, frequency, and cost terms
in `[AUDIT CLAUSE / SCHEDULE]`.

Regulatory audits required by applicable law remain subject to applicable law.
Audit rights do not authorize access to other customers' data, trade secrets, or
security information beyond what is necessary and appropriately protected.

## 10. International transfers

The Parties will not transfer personal data to a third country or international
organization unless the requirements of applicable law are met. The applicable
transfer mechanism, assessment, safeguards, and supplementary measures are
described in Schedule 5.

No schedule entry is a representation that a transfer mechanism is valid or
sufficient in every circumstance. The Parties will reassess transfers after
legal, technical, or subprocessor changes.

## 11. Return, deletion, and retention

At the end of the relevant services, Processor will, at Controller's choice,
return or securely delete personal data and existing copies, unless applicable
law requires storage. Any required retention will be limited to
`[PERIOD / PURPOSE]`, protected from further processing, and deleted when the
requirement ends.

Deletion from backups may occur according to
`[BACKUP ROTATION / TECHNICAL PROCESS]`. Processor will provide a deletion
confirmation at `[METHOD]` when requested and technically possible.

## 12. Records and accountability

Each Party will maintain records and supporting evidence required by applicable
law and the Agreement. Processor will make relevant information available to
Controller through `[REPORTING METHOD / FREQUENCY]`.

## 13. Liability and precedence

The Parties' liability, indemnification, insurance, and remedy terms are
governed by `[MASTER AGREEMENT CLAUSE]`, subject to mandatory law. If this DPA
conflicts with the Agreement regarding data-protection obligations, this DPA
controls for the specific conflict to the extent permitted by law.

## 14. Term, suspension, and termination

This DPA begins on the Effective Date and continues while Processor processes
personal data for Controller. Either Party may suspend affected processing when
necessary to address a material legal or security risk. Termination of the
Agreement ends this DPA except for provisions that must survive, including
confidentiality, security, audit, transfer, deletion, and liability provisions
as applicable.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Party roles and the scope of processing are confirmed.
- [ ] Every schedule is complete, accurate, and consistent with the service.
- [ ] Instructions, retention, deletion, and backup behavior are specific.
- [ ] Security measures are evidenced and reviewed at an appropriate frequency.
- [ ] Subprocessors, locations, and transfer safeguards are current.
- [ ] Breach contacts, timing, and escalation paths are tested.
- [ ] Audit rights are workable and do not expose unrelated customer data.
- [ ] Liability, precedence, suspension, and termination terms are aligned with
      the master agreement.
- [ ] Mandatory legal and sector-specific requirements received qualified
      review.
- [ ] Signed copies and version history are retained.
